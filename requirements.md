# Movie Theater Ticket Kiosk Requirements

## Requirement 1 — View Movies and Showtimes
A customer can view the available movies and their available showtimes.

## Requirement 2 — Select Seat
A customer can choose an available seat for a selected showtime.

## Requirement 3 — Purchase Ticket
A customer can purchase a ticket for the selected movie, showtime, and seat.

## Requirement 4 — Confirmation
The system provides a confirmation after a successful ticket purchase.

## Requirement 5 — Prevent Duplicate Seat Sales
The system must prevent the same seat from being sold twice for the same showtime.
